FAQ

How long does RTU take to respond?

Typical response is under 2 hours. Busy periods may take longer, but we always acknowledge your request.

What info should I include in my message?

Please include your name, order/account ID (if any), issue description, and screenshots where helpful.

How do refunds work?

Refunds depend on product type and policy. Submit a request and we’ll confirm eligibility and next steps.

Can I escalate a complaint?

Yes. If unresolved, we escalate to a supervisor and provide an update timeline.
